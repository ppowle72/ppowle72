Can We Eat Almonds in Fast? Vrat Me Badam Kha Sakte Hai? (Navratri, Hindu & Intermittent Fasting Guide)

By Prashant Powle, Powle Home Foods (GI Authorised User AU/5974/GI/139/260) · Last updated 21 October 2026 · 6 min read

Yes, you can eat almonds in fast. Vrat me badam kha sakte hai — almonds (badam) are allowed in Navratri, Ekadashi, Shivratri and most Hindu fasts, because they are a plain, unprocessed Sattvic food. In intermittent fasting the answer flips: almonds are fine in your eating window, but any almond eaten during the fasting window technically breaks the fast.

Quick Answers: Almonds and Fasting

  • Navratri: Yes. Plain badam is allowed on all nine days.
  • Ekadashi, Shivratri, Janmashtami: Yes. Dry fruits are widely taken in falahari fasts.
  • Does badam break a vrat? No, a vrat is not broken by almonds (badam se vrat nahi tutta). Grain, pulses and regular salt are what most vrats avoid.
  • Badam shake in vrat: Yes, if made with only milk, almonds and sugar, honey or a fruit. Skip any mix with non-fasting additives.
  • Salted almonds: Only if salted with sendha namak (rock salt).
  • Intermittent fasting: Not in the fasting window. Yes in the eating window.

Fasting rules differ by family, region and sampradaya. If your family follows a stricter vrat (nirjala, or no dry fruits), follow that.

1. Almonds in Hindu Religious Fasting (Vrat / Upvas)

In Hindu tradition, vrat usually means avoiding grains, pulses and regular salt, while fruits, nuts and milk are generally taken. Almonds are widely treated as Sattvic food: simple, natural and supportive of health and strength. The Bhagavad Gita (chapter 17, verse 8) describes Sattvic foods as those that increase life, strength and well-being, and Ayurveda has long recommended badam, usually soaked, for energy and nourishment.

Navratri Vrat Me Badam (नवरात्रि में बादाम)

Almonds are a staple of the nine days of Navratri. They give devotees who avoid grains a steady source of fats, protein and fibre. Most people prefer soaked almonds in the morning, which are softer and easier to digest. Navratri me badam kha sakte hai — yes, soaked or dry, both are fine.

Ekadashi, Shivratri and Janmashtami

Dry fruits including badam are commonly eaten raw or used in falahari recipes during these fasts. They help keep energy up through a long day without cooking.

Upvas Me Badam Kha Sakte Hai? (उपवास में बादाम)

Yes. In a regular upvas almonds do not break the fast. They are a better choice than fried or packaged "vrat snacks" because they are whole and unprocessed.

2. Can You Drink Badam Shake in Vrat? (बादाम शेक व्रत में)

Badam shake vrat me pi sakte hai? Yes, in most Hindu fasts a badam shake is acceptable. It does not break a vrat, as long as it contains only:

  • Milk (or a vrat-friendly milk alternative)
  • Soaked, peeled almonds
  • A natural sweetener such as sugar, mishri, honey or dates
  • Optional: cardamom or saffron

Avoid ready-made mixes, flavoured powders and added colours, since they often contain ingredients that are not vrat-friendly.

Simple Vrat Badam Shake Recipe

  1. Soak 8–10 almonds overnight and peel them.
  2. Blend with 1 cup cold milk, 1 tsp mishri or 2 dates and a pinch of cardamom.
  3. Serve fresh. Add saffron strands for Navratri.

4. Almonds and Intermittent Fasting (IF)

Intermittent fasting is a different kind of fast. It is about timing, not religious rules. Almonds are calorie-dense: a small handful of about 23 almonds (28 g) has around 160 calories (USDA FoodData Central), so even a few almonds provide energy and will end a strict fast.

  • Fasting window: Avoid almonds. If your goal is a strict zero-calorie fast, stick to water, black coffee or plain tea.
  • Eating window: Almonds are a good choice. Their protein, fibre and healthy fats help you feel full, and they have a low glycaemic impact, which suits the meal after a fast.

Why Almonds Work Well While Fasting

  1. Steady energy: Fats and protein release energy slowly, so there is less of a mid-day crash than with sugary snacks.
  2. Fullness: Fibre and protein help control hunger pangs.
  3. Heart-friendly fats: Almonds are rich in monounsaturated fats, which support heart health as part of a balanced diet.
  4. Key nutrients: Vitamin E, magnesium and protein, which fasting days can run short on.

5. How Many Almonds to Eat in a Fast?

A common serving is 5–10 soaked almonds in the morning, and up to about 20 across the whole day for adults. If you are fasting for many days (like Navratri), spread them out: a few in the morning, some with fruit in the afternoon and a badam shake in the evening. People with nut allergies, kidney stones or on medication should check with a doctor. See our full guide: how many almonds to eat daily.

6. Best Ways to Eat Almonds While Fasting

  • Soaked and peeled: Soak overnight for easier digestion.
  • With fruit: Pair with banana, apple or papaya for a falahari plate.
  • Badam shake or badam milk: See the recipe above.
  • In vrat sweets: Chopped in kheer made with sabudana or makhana.
  • Mixed dry fruit bowl: Almonds, dates, walnuts and raisins.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can we eat almonds during Navratri fast? / नवरात्रि में बादाम खा सकते हैं?

Yes. Almonds are considered Sattvic and are allowed throughout Navratri. They help maintain energy levels when you are avoiding grains.

Vrat me badam kha sakte hai? / व्रत में बादाम खा सकते हैं?

Yes. Almonds can be eaten in vrat, soaked or dry. They do not break a vrat.

Does eating almonds break a vrat? / बादाम से व्रत टूटता है क्या?

No. In Hindu vrat, almonds do not break the fast. Only foods your own tradition forbids, such as grains or regular salt, do.

Can we drink badam shake in vrat? / बादाम शेक व्रत में पी सकते हैं?

Yes, in most fasts. Make it with milk, soaked almonds and natural sweetener only, and avoid ready-made mixes.

Can we eat kaju badam in upvas? / उपवास में काजू बादाम खा सकते हैं?

Yes. Cashews and almonds are both commonly eaten in fasts like Navratri, Ekadashi and Shivratri.

Does eating an almond break intermittent fasting?

Yes, if eaten during your fasting window, because almonds contain calories. Save them for your eating window.

Are salted almonds allowed in Hindu fasts?

Only if salted with sendha namak (rock salt). Regular table salt is usually avoided in traditional Hindu fasts.

How many almonds can I eat during a fast?

About 5–10 soaked almonds in the morning, and up to roughly 20 across the day for most healthy adults.

Should almonds be soaked for vrat?

Soaking is optional but popular. Soaked and peeled almonds are softer and easier to digest, which helps on an empty stomach.
