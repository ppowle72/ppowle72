## Cautions
1. **Value is low.** The 26 linking pages sit on topic-farm sites (topicsdaily, buzzcenter, readerspool and similar, DR mostly under 15). Three are flagged spam, and four are already lost. Do not describe them as "featured in".
2. **Look-alike names.** About 100 of the 197 are .co.in or .in domains named like "Times of India Daily", "Indian Express Daily", "India Today Daily". They are not those publications. Never show their logos or names as media coverage on the site.
3. **Claims in the release conflict with the homepage.** The release says "over 22,000 pincodes", "Over 35,000 Plus customers", "past five years" and "over 100 locations nationwide" with delivery to the UAE, UK, USA and others. The homepage says 12,000+ customers, and Shopify shows 5,896 customers and 8,215 orders in three years. Do not copy the release numbers onto the homepage unless you can support them. Also check the Vedic "harvested at 3:20 AM" and "Engineer and Doctor founders" claims before reusing them.
4. **Duplicate content.** The same article on 197 sites is syndicated duplicate content; Google will mostly ignore it. Real gains come from the few genuine outlets (ANI, ThePrint, LatestLY, mid-day).
