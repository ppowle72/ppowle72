About AlphonsoMango.in

AlphonsoMango.in is a Mumbai-based seller of GI-tagged Ratnagiri and Devgad Alphonso (Hapus) mangoes. Fruit is sold by the dozen, ripened without carbide, and delivered across India. The business is run by Powle Home Foods, a registered GI Authorised User for Alphonso mango, with an FSSAI licence and an APEDA export registration.
